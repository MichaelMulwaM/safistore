About SafiStore

Empowering sustainable living and local craftsmanship across Kenya.

Our Mission

At SafiStore, we believe that everyday choices have a lasting impact. Our mission is to make sustainable, plastic-free living accessible by offering durable, handcrafted alternatives that replace single-use plastics in Kenyan households.

Our Roots & Craftsmanship

Founded with a passion for environmental conservation, we partner directly with local Kenyan artisans and weavers. From traditional handwoven kiondos to natural sisal scrubbers and organic kitchenware, every product tells a story of heritage, skill, and environmental responsibility.

Why Choose Us?

100% Eco-Friendly

Carefully sourced natural materials designed to minimize waste and protect our environment.

Local Artisans

Supporting community weavers and local makers by promoting authentic Kenyan craftsmanship.

Built to Last

Durable, high-quality alternatives built to replace disposable items for years to come.
